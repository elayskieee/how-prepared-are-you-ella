How Prepared Are You? is an educational puzzle game designed to help people learn how to prepare for calamities and emergencies I created this game to raise awareness about emergency preparedness, and help players understand the importance of making smart decisions when unexpected situations happen.

Through multiple-choice questions, emergency supply challenges, problem-solving activities, (and a bonus round), players can test their knowledge while having fun. The game encourages everyone to think critically, choose wisely, and learn what to do before, during, and after a calamity.

My goal is to help people become more prepared, think smarter, and make responsible decisions that can protect themselves, their families, and their pets during emergencies. Because being prepared is not just about having supplies, it's about knowing what to do when it matters most.
